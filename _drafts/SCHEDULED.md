## Notes

- **MWF is the default cadence.** April's daily burst was a one-time relaunch push — not sustainable long-term.
- **May–June use Sun/Mon fills** for series that benefit from tighter spacing (ZFS 3-part, Draft Preview 3-part).
- **July onward returns to strict MWF** unless a series needs consecutive days.
- **Substack retrospectives** (mid-month) and **LinkedIn posts** are not tracked here — see `_substack/README.md` and `_linkedin/README.md`.
- **Q4 is intentionally open.** New content will emerge from the K8s-on-Proxmox project, EMBA coursework, and whatever breaks in the homelab next.
- **Tier 4/5 drafts** (raw, hold, superseded) are not scheduled. They'll either get promoted when ready or archived.
- When promoting a draft, update all four date fields (filename, `date:`, `seo.date_published`, `seo.date_modified`) and move to `_posts/`.
